# Mercator development

Work locally by default. Do not deploy, publish, upload source or call Sites hosting tools unless the user explicitly requests publication. Keep a local Vite preview available.

Build independent educational scenes, then connect them into an atlas. Keep scene metadata, mathematical rules, navigation and rendered assets separate. Preserve historical mathematical connectivity; distinguish illustrative reconstruction from documented history.

Run the graph/navigation tests and production build after changes to gameplay. Verify camera and movement interactions in the browser when repairing them.

Graphics quality is a priority. Inspect assets at close range as well as the overall composition. Avoid intersecting contrast-coloured primitives in foliage, disconnected particle-only waterfalls, and lighting that washes out form. Preserve continuous silhouettes, intentional material colours, readable shadows and stable camera controls. Keep reusable assets in src/scenes/assets.
