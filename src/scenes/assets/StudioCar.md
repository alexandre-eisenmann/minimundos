# 1974 studio sports car

`StudioCar.tsx` is a hand-built, Countach LP400-inspired mesh. It is an illustrative prize, not a claim about a car offered on a particular television broadcast or a dimensionally exact automotive replica.

The design reference is the early LP400: low wedge bonnet, narrow roof, unextended wheel arches, five-hole alloys, side air inlets and rear cooling boxes. Lamborghini dates this version to 1974–1978 in [its production retrospective](https://www.lamborghini.com/de-en/nachrichten/50-years-ago-the-first-countach-in-santagata-bolognese).

The component owns body panels, glazing, wheel-well strips, trim and mechanical details. Local +X points toward the nose; its wrapper preserves the existing booth orientation and scale. Geometry and the registration texture are disposed on unmount. No downloaded models, textures or fonts are required.

## Local inspection

Run the Vite development server and open `/car-preview.html`. The development-only HTML entry provides front, rear and side views with orbit and zoom controls, procedural studio reflections and shadows. It is not a production build entry.

Check the actual game at `/scenes/monty-hall`: complete a round to reveal the prizes. The booth uses its existing studio lights and car scale.
